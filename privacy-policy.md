**Privacy Policy**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Photo to Paint application collects, uses, and protects user information.

### Information We Collect

* Photos uploaded by users
* Basic device information for app performance and crash reporting
* Purchase and subscription status information

### How We Use Information

* To convert uploaded photos into coloring pages
* To improve app performance and user experience
* To manage subscriptions and purchases

### Photo and Image Data

Uploaded photos are used only for generating coloring pages.
Photos are not shared with third parties and are not used for advertising.
Images may be stored temporarily during processing and are deleted after processing is complete.

### Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for a general audience, including children.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Parents or legal guardians are responsible for supervising app usage.

### Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services for the following purposes:

* Payments and subscriptions
* Analytics and crash reporting

These services process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

### Data Security

Reasonable security measures are taken to protect user data from unauthorized access or misuse.

### Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
Continued use of the app means acceptance of the updated policy.
